# First Three Minutes

**An offline-capable, single-file emergency first-aid quick reference.**

Open [`index.html`](index.html) in any browser. No build step, no dependencies, no network required. Save it to your phone, print it for the fridge, or host it anywhere.

## Why this exists

This project was built to a simple prompt: *"the greatest good for the greatest number of people."*

Few pieces of knowledge have a higher value-per-byte than knowing what to do in the first three minutes of a medical emergency — before the ambulance arrives:

- Bystander CPR **doubles or triples** survival from cardiac arrest, yet most bystanders do nothing or compress at the wrong rate.
- Stroke treatment is measured in minutes; recognizing F.A.S.T. signs early preserves brain.
- Naloxone reverses opioid overdoses, but only if the person next to the victim knows the signs.
- Most of these emergencies happen **at home**, to people we love.

A page you can save once and consult in a panic — large text, numbered steps, no jargon, works offline — is a small artifact with an outsized ceiling on the good it can do.

## Important disclaimer

This is a **memory aid**, not medical advice, and **not a substitute for certified first-aid training** or for the instructions of an emergency dispatcher. Content follows widely published lay-rescuer guidance (American Heart Association, IFRC, UK NHS, and similar), simplified for untrained bystanders. Guidance evolves — verify against current sources, and better yet, take a first-aid course.
